1. LOGICAL WORKLOAD DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of splitting Frontend vs Backend, we are dividing the 10 Phases into two 
logically grouped business domains. Both developers will work as Full-Stack 
engineers, completing both the UI and APIs for their assigned phases.

- DEVELOPER 1 (Commercial & Admin Focus): 
  Responsible for Auth, CRM, Bookings, Finance, Procurement, HR, and Chat.
  
- DEVELOPER 2 (Operations & Inventory Focus):
  Responsible for Warehouses, Inventory, Reservations, Dispatch, and Returns.

================================================================================
4. DEPENDENCY & COLLABORATION PLAN
================================================================================
Since both developers are building interconnected systems, they must collaborate at 
specific intersection points:

1. Stock Availability Check (Phase 5 meets Phase 3/6):
   - Dev 1 is building Quotations. Dev 2 is building Inventory/Reservations.
   - Dev 1 will call Dev 2's Stock Check Service when generating a quote.

2. Invoice Generation (Phase 10 meets Phase 5):
   - Dev 1 builds Finance/Invoices based on the Booking data they built in Phase 5, 
     but must also account for any Damaged items marked by Dev 2 in Phase 8.

3. Purchase to Inventory (Phase 9 meets Phase 3):
   - Dev 1 builds Vendor Purchase Orders. When Goods are received, they trigger 
     an inventory update using Dev 2's Item Master services.
