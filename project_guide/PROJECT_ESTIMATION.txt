1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ESTIMATION OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is an Enterprise-Grade Warehouse-Centric Event Management ERP designed
for single-owner tent and event management operations with 4 godowns. 

The scope covers 10 Comprehensive Phases, 30 Backend REST API Modules, 31 Frontend
Services, grouped UI components across 8 business departments, 16 ERP Reports, 
Real-Time Stock Reservations, Damage/Repair/Scrap Workflows, Real-Time Team Chat Messenger
with WhatsApp-Style Voice Note Recording, and Financial P&L.

ESTIMATION SUMMARY:
- Total Engineering & UI Development:  578 Hours
- System Integration & End-to-End QA:     46 Hours
- Bug Fixing, Edge Cases & Polishing:    46 Hours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOTAL PROJECT HOURS REQUIRED:           670 HOURS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
