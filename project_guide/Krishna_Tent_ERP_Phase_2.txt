Business Rules
- No negative stock.
- Reserved stock cannot be booked again.
- Every movement creates an inventory ledger entry.
- Every dispatch belongs to an Event and Warehouse.
- Partial dispatch and partial return are supported.
- Damage, repair and missing stock require remarks and approval.
- Warehouse transfer updates both source and destination stock.
