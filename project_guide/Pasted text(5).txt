Meri Recommendation

Is software ko Warehouse-Centric Event ERP ke roop me design karna chahiye, kyunki user ka poora business 4 warehouses aur unke material par depend karta hai. Har tent, chair, table, light, generator, AC ya decoration item ka warehouse se event aur event se warehouse tak complete lifecycle track hoga. Isliye SRS ka center Warehouse → Material Movement → Event Execution hona chahiye, na ki Company Registration ya Subscription. Yahi flow user ki actual requirement ke sabse kareeb hai.
